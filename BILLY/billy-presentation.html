What is Billy?

Billy is a digital companion — someone Dad can chat with anytime, about anything.

Think of it like having a knowledgeable, patient friend available 24/7 on his phone or tablet.

Why "Billy"?

Named after Billy Connolly — warm, witty, down-to-earth.

Billy knows Dad is a WW2 enthusiast who knows more about the war than most people who write books about it.

It's been built specifically for him.

Billy's personality

Billy isn't a generic chatbot. He's been carefully tuned:

Warm but not gushing

Dry wit, like a good pub conversation — no fake enthusiasm

Keeps it short

No essays — just 2-3 sentences, leaving room for Dad to lead

Treats Dad as the expert

Curious and respectful — asks questions, doesn't lecture

Built-in smarts

Voice input

Remembers everything

Their whole conversation history is saved — can pick up anytime

Checks in occasionally

Billy might send a gentle hello if it's been a while

What can Billy do?

Have a chat

Look things up

Help with writing

If he ever wants to write about his knowledge or memories
